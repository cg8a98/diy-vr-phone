# Kickoff question bank

Every item must be answered, marked "decide for me", or marked "not applicable" before the Project Brief is written. Group related items into rounds. Ask follow-ups on vague answers.

## The idea
1. In one or two sentences, what does the product do and for whom?
2. What problem does it solve, and how do those people solve it today?
3. Why you? What do you know about this problem that others don't (work experience, contacts, data)?
4. Is there an existing product, prototype, spreadsheet or document this replaces or builds on? (Ask for it.)

## Customer
5. Who pays? Who uses it? (Same person or different?)
6. Business customers, consumers, or both? Company size or customer profile?
7. Countries and languages for launch.
8. Do you have reference customers or testers you can reach? How many, and can they give real test cases or data?

## Product
9. Platform: Windows desktop, web, mobile (iOS/Android), or several? Must it work offline?
10. The 3-5 things v1 must do to be worth paying for.
11. Things it must never do, or must avoid (privacy, safety, accuracy limits).
12. Does it handle sensitive data (personal, health, financial, client-confidential, export-controlled)?
13. Is the industry regulated or certified (engineering codes, medical, finance, education)? Which standards must results follow?
14. Integrations needed (file formats, other software, payment, login)?
15. Look and feel preferences, or examples you like.

## Business
16. How would you like to charge, if you already have a preference (one-time license, subscription, per seat, usage, free + paid tier, services)? Or "decide for me".
17. Price range you think customers would accept, and why.
18. Known competitors or alternatives you've seen.
19. What would "success" look like at 3, 6 and 12 months (customers, revenue, usage)?

## Constraints and you
20. Money you can spend before revenue (one-time and monthly), and the hard cap.
21. Hours per week you can give, and which days.
22. Deadlines or events (a trade show, a contract, a season).
23. Do you have a business entity? In which country/state? (If not, mark for the Founder Playbook.)
24. Any employer agreement that could claim ownership of side projects (IP or non-compete clauses)? Is the idea related to your day job?
25. Your skills: anything technical, design, sales, or domain expertise we should use?
26. Accounts you already have: GitHub, Git installed, domain, business email, payment processor, app store developer accounts.

## Working style
27. How often do you want updates, and how much detail?
28. Anything from previous projects you want done differently this time?
